WearBy Privacy Policy

This policy describes information processed when you use WearBy and the choices available to you.

Last updated: October 19, 2026

WearBy is a neighborhood marketplace for clothing offered for sale or rent. This policy applies to the WearBy mobile app and related services. For general information, visit www.wearby.com.pk.

Information we process

  • Account and profile details: name, email address, optional building code, profile photo, and information needed to create and maintain your account.
  • Marketplace content: listings, item photos and details, prices, sizes, condition, comments, chat text and voice messages, ratings, and reports you submit.
  • Location: if you grant location permission, the app uses device location to show nearby listings and calculate approximate distances. Exact coordinates are not displayed to other members. A rounded location may be stored for nearby-ad alerts and expires from matching after 30 days. You can deny or revoke location permission in your device settings; nearby features may then be limited.
  • Push notifications: if you enable notifications, a device push token is stored to deliver alerts to your device. You can manage notification permissions in device settings.
  • Service and safety information: service providers may process technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the app. Reports and block records are processed to support community safety.

How we use information

Information is used to create and manage accounts, show local listings, enable comments and conversations, provide approximate distance and relevant alerts, support safety tools, and maintain and troubleshoot WearBy.

What other members can see

Your profile details and listing content are shown to members as needed for marketplace features. Comments are public within the relevant listing. Private chats and voice messages are available to participants in that conversation. WearBy does not show your exact address to other members.

Service providers and sharing

WearBy uses service providers, including Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage, and Expo notification services to deliver push notifications. Google sign-in providers may process information needed to authenticate you. These providers process information to provide app services. We do not sell personal information. We may also retain or disclose information where reasonably necessary to protect people, enforce our terms, or comply with applicable law.

Retention and account deletion

We keep information for as long as needed to provide WearBy and address legitimate safety, legal, and operational needs. You can permanently delete your account from Profile in the app. Deletion removes your profile, listings and photos, comments, chats and voice notes, ratings, and push tokens. Reports about a deleted account may be retained to address safety, legal, or dispute concerns. Deletion may not immediately remove copies held in provider backups.

Your choices and account security

You can edit your profile, block members, report content, and manage location and notification permissions. We use access controls provided by our service providers, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your sign-in credentials private and contact us if you believe your account is being used without permission.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as WearBy changes. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated date. For privacy questions, visit www.wearby.com.pk.

Please review this policy against your current app behavior, service providers, and legal obligations before publication.
