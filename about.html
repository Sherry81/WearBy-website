A little more local

Clothes can bring a neighborhood closer.

WearBy helps people nearby give clothes another life—by finding pieces to buy, renting something for a special day, or passing along what they no longer wear.

Why WearBy

Good finds are even better close to home.

Clothes often sit unworn in one closet while someone nearby is looking for just that piece. WearBy brings local listings and neighbor-to-neighbor conversations together in one place.

Because people meet in person, they can see and check an item before deciding. The buyer and seller or renter make the arrangement directly—WearBy does not arrange shipping or process payments.

Our goal is simple: make it easier for good clothes to get worn again and for neighbors to connect along the way.

A marketplace with a human touch

Nearby by design. Neighborly by nature.

WearBy is built around local discovery and direct conversations, not parcels and checkout screens.
